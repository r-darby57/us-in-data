How to export your messages
iMessage (Mac)

Use a desktop tool — iMazing or the imessage-exporter CLI. Export the chat as CSV with columns date, sender, message.

WhatsApp

Open the chat → tap the contact → Export Chat → Without Media → save the .txt or .zip.

Generic CSV

Headers anywhere — we'll detect them. Expected columns: date, sender, message. Example row:

2024-03-14T21:14:00,Ryan,sweet dreams ❤️
